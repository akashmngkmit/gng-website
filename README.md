# Groove & Grub

**Groove & Grub** is a stunning, high-energy & trendiest food truck in Kochi, Kerala.

## About the Business

**Groove & Grub** isn't just about food; it's about the vibe. Stationed at the scenic **Marine Drive in Kochi**, we serve gourmet street food accompanied by the best beats in town. 

* **Location:** Marine Drive (Near the Rainbow Bridge), Kochi, Kerala.
* **Vibe:** Sunsets, Backwaters, Lo-fi Beats, and Spicy Treats.
* **Specialty:** Fusion Kerala Street Food & Continental Smashed Burgers.


## Tech Stack

* **HTML5:** Semantic structure.
* **CSS3:** Flexbox, Grid, CSS Variables, Media Queries.
* **Dev Tools:** Chrome Developer Tools used for debugging and layout testing.
