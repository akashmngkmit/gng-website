Our Story

Welcome to Groove & Grub, where the beats are as fresh as the ingredients. Stationed at the heart of Marine Drive, we bring a fusion of global street food and local Kerala flavors straight to your plate.

It started with a simple idea: good food tastes better with good music. Whether you're here for our signature smash burgers or just to vibe with the sunset, we promise an experience that feeds your soul.

Fresh & Local

Farm to Street Concept

We don't just cook; we craft. Every morning, our team visits the local markets of Ernakulam to hand-pick the freshest produce. From the crunchy lettuce in your burger to the spices in our secret sauce, everything is locally sourced.

No frozen patties. No artificial preservatives. Just honest food made with passion and served with a smile.

The Groove Experience

Signature Smashes

Our famous double-patty smash burger, seared to perfection. Topped with melted cheddar, caramelized onions, and our secret house sauce on a toasted brioche bun.

Live Beats

Good food deserves a great soundtrack. Enjoy curated lo-fi sets and live acoustic performances every weekend right by the bay.

Late Night Vibes

Open until 1 AM for your midnight cravings. Join the community under the stars for the best street food experience in Kochi.
